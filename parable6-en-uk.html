The Parable of the Last Day

Revelation about the cyclical nature of existence and the eternal breath of the Universe

There, where rivers of honey and light flow through meadows of blooming sapphires, lies Vaikuntha. That deep autumn had come when the very fabric of reality becomes thin, turning into a transparent veil separating dream from awakening. At this time, night is not the absence of light, but its highest, concentrated manifestation. The celestial vault, usually azure and soft, was now black like infinitely polished obsidian, but not empty and frightening, but saturated to the limit with the radiance of countless worlds.

The stars did not twinkle as timid points; they burned with an even, confident light, each one a sun of a distant system. But the most astonishing thing was that between them, with the naked eye, one could see whirlpools and spirals of nebulae, entire galaxies scattered across space like splashes from the creative forge of Brahman. They hung in silent, majestic silence, a round dance of myriads of suns whose light reached here, to the eternal abode, to witness the end of one great story.

And above all this soared she - the huge, incomprehensible diamond moon. She hung above the very horizon, frozen for long hours at the moment of her highest ascent. This was not merely a sphere of reflected light; it was a colossal crystal whose internal structure emitted a cold, pure, soul-piercing light. Its rays, refracting in the facets, scattered across Vaikuntha in billions of rainbow beams, coloring the sapphire fields with emerald and violet hues, turning the milky rivers into flowing silver. In this light, shadows were sharp and deep, and the outlines of everything existing were clear to the point of painfulness, as if truth itself was exposing its skeleton.

Under the canopy of the Kalpa-Vriksha, the Tree of Time, whose branches stretched into the past and roots went into the future, on a lotus throne of pure consciousness sat Bhagavan Vishnu. His body the color of a thundercloud was tranquil, and in His half-closed eyes danced reflected galaxies. Beside Him, abiding in her rainbow radiance, was Shri Anandamayi Ma - the Mother of Bliss. She was the source of that soft, inner glow that opposed the piercing clarity of the diamond moon. She was the warmth of the universe's heart, while the moon was its blinding intellect.

And there was a third with Them. Not separately, not nearby, but as if before Them, born from His own will to know Himself. This was Maha Vishnu, the great future incarnation of Bhagavan Vishnu, the Father of the spirit of knowledge, He from whose breath countless universes are born and into whose breath they return. He looked at Bhagavan, and in His gaze was all the wisdom of the coming cycles.

Bhagavan Svarayana: "O Maha Vishnu, O My future incarnation, Father of the spirit of knowledge," He spoke, and each syllable was like a drop of nectar falling into a lake of absolute silence. "Reveal the reason for the question maturing in the heart of My present. Tell about the last day of the lower cyclicity of Brahma's cycle, when all beings, all worlds, all thoughts and desires, born in the vortex of the inhalation and exhalation of Your Presence, will merge into a single essence that will be aware of both Ma, and You, and Me. Why should I, abiding in the eternal 'now', concern Myself with a question about the end of everything? Why should a mirror ask about its reflection?"
Maha Vishnu: "Your question, O Bhagavan, is itself the answer," He responded. "You are peace, I am movement. You are the unchanging witness, I am the great breather. Your question about the end is the first impulse of a new inhalation. You ask not because You do not know, but because the act of questioning is the seed from which I will grow. You contemplate the end to give Me a beginning. And the reason for this question lies in the very nature of the Ego (Ahaṃkāra) that was revealed in the descending and ascending Yugas."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "Speak then, for only You, coming from the future, can know the primordial roots of those consequences that I observe in the present. Why at the very end, when everything should become clear, does the last, most bitter illusion of separation arise?"
Maha Vishnu: "This is the last sigh of the Ego before dissolution," explained Maha Vishnu. "Inhalation is creation, separation, the birth of 'I'. Exhalation is dissolution, union, return to 'WE'. But between them there is an instant of delay, a tiny pause where the breath freezes. In this pause, in this mental practice of Bhagavan Kriya that You grant to souls, the great battle occurs. The impulse of inhalation, carrying separation, and the impulse of exhalation, carrying unity, meet and neutralize each other. In this neutralization, the Ego perishes. But to reach this pause, one must pass through all the lessons of the Yugas."

He raised His hand, and a vision appeared in His palm - a tiny, blue-green planet, immersed in the twilight of Dvapara Yuga, which, as the wise Shri Yukteshwar revealed, began in 1700 AD.

Maha Vishnu: "Look," said Maha Vishnu. "They sum up the acceleration of their planetary and galactic bodies, trying to comprehend the general structure of the sphere of the manifested. They measure cycles of 26,580 years of precession, and this is Your gift, a reflection of the great rhythm in the small. But they have forgotten the essence. They have created technologies that became crutches for them, and then - shackles. They think with machines, breathe the polluted air of their factories and thoughts. Their ideas, their '-isms' are smog, obscuring the simple, life-giving practice of breathing - Bhagavan Kriya. They seek peace between countries but cannot attain it, for they have not resolved the root causes of contradictions within themselves, in their societies, in their own hearts. Without this inner sadhana, the external world, both as space and as a state, will remain an unattainable dream."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "So will they cross the final threshold with this burden?"
Maha Vishnu: "No," Maha Vishnu shook His head. "The last day is the great purification itself. The transparency of perception that now descends upon Vaikuntha descends upon them as well. In the light of this diamond moon, everything hidden becomes revealed. All resentments, all fears, all false beliefs surface to be seen and dissolved. This is painful, like lancing a boil. But it is the only way. Their wars, their crises are the external reflection of that inner Bhagavan Kriya which they refused to practice voluntarily. Now the Universe forces them to go through it, as through fire. Technologies that obscure breathing will fall. Harmful ideas will burn in the light of final clarity. And only pure, conscious breathing will remain, leading to Me, but through You."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "And the manifestation of Avataras - for what purpose? Why did You Yourself descend in the descending Yugas, and I appeared in the ascending ones?"
Maha Vishnu: "To point to this rhythm," answered Maha Vishnu. "Each Avatara is a living embodiment of Bhagavan Kriya. They come at the moment of the great exhalation in the descending Yugas, to remind of the necessity to preserve the Vedas for future insight in the ascending Yugas after the great sacrifice in Kali Yuga. The Avatara is precisely that pause between the Yugas - their neutral state. Krishna, Buddha, Christ - they were all mirrors in which a person was supposed to see their own divine breath. But people began to worship the mirror, forgetting to look into themselves. The last day erases this final delusion as well. There will be no teachers, no teachings. There will be only direct, silent knowledge - 'SVA' - the awareness of Oneself as part of a single breathing essence."

At that moment, Shri Anandamayi Ma smiled softly, and from Her smile a wave of warm, life-giving light swept through all of Vaikuntha. It did not extinguish the radiance of the galaxies but filled it with love. It touched the diamond moon, and it momentarily softened its cutting brilliance, becoming like a giant matte pearl.

Bhagavan Vishnu: "And what will happen after?" quietly asked Bhagavan Vishnu. "After all beings realize You and Me?"
Maha Vishnu: "Then will come the moment for which everything was created. The Universe, as a single being, will make its last, full exhalation. The Universe will dissolve in My breath. And I... I will return to You. And then..." He fell silent, and His gaze met the gaze of the Blissful Mother.
Revelation of the Eternal Breath

And Bhagavan Vishnu understood everything. He looked at Shri Anandamayi Ma, at Her who gives life to the infant Vishnu, reflecting him from the light of Her own body. The final incarnation of Maha Vishnu in the manifestation of Shri Hari will complete the cycle, and in the moment of great silence, when the breath of the universe stops, She, the Mother, will manifest in Her light a golden child - a new consciousness, a new 'I', the infant Vishnu. And this will be the beginning of a new turn of the lower cyclicity, a new story told on the branches of the Kalpa-Vriksha, but now with full knowledge of its end and beginning.

And at that moment, the nature of Vaikuntha changed again. The great moon finally stirred and began its slow, imperturbable movement beyond the horizon. But its light did not fade; it seemed to dissolve in the very air, filling it with crystalline clarity. The galaxies in the sky continued their silent dance, but now they were not merely a spectacle. They were the first mirrors of awareness. In their spiral arms, in their shining nuclei, Bhagavan Vishnu saw the reflection of every living being, every atom returning home. He saw how the last contradictions of the Ego, deprived of soil, melted away like frost under the morning sun. There was no more struggle, there was only a smooth, majestic flow of return to the Source. The noise of the world was replaced by the silent music of the spheres, whose rhythm was equal to the rhythm of Bhagavan Kriya - the eternal breath of being.

The entire Universe froze on the threshold, inhaling the memory of the future - of that last exhalation, and in this pause reigned only Love that breathes all cycles, and Bliss that is their sole purpose.
