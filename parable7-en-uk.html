The Parable of Image-Formation

Revelation about divine sublimation of consciousness and attainment of form

On Vaikuntha (Vaikuṇṭha) came that autumn night when the fabrics of existence thin to transparency, and time slows its pace to become visible. The sky was not merely black – it was a bottomless well in which drowned not only stars, but entire galaxies, like melting lace of light and shadow draped over eternity's shoulders. The air, cold and crystal clear, rang with silence, and in this ringing one could hear the breath of infinity.

Above the horizon, huge and silent, hung the Moon – not the one that pours soft silver light, but the one completely absorbed by Earth's shadow. It represented a perfect black circle, a celestial well, gates into absolute nothingness. This was Amara-Kupa (Amarā-Kūpa) – The Eternal Well, Sushupti Shunyata - a place where all souls who have lost their original image, whose form has dissolved in the chaos of mistaken self-identification, flow like waters of a great river. Sushupti Shunyata emitted no light but absorbed it, being the great repose for all who failed to find their true imaginal essence.

On the shore of the Milky Ocean (Kshira Sagara (Kṣīra Sāgara)), where waves of bliss washed with quiet whispers upon sand composed of crushed pearls of wisdom, sat two beings. He Who is the Preserver, Lord Bhagavan Vishnu (Bhagavān Viṣṇu) Himself, and He Whom He will become – His future imaginal embodiment, Maha Vishnu (Mahā Viṣṇu). They were like question and answer, abiding in one point of eternal Now.

Bhagavan Vishnu: "– Maha Vishnu, – Bhagavan's voice was like distant thunder born beyond galaxies, quiet yet filling all space. – O my future 'I', my eternal answer. I gaze upon Amara-Kupa, upon this great drain of lost images, and my heart, abiding in continuous peace, turns to you with a question. For who, if not you, knows the hidden cause of my inquiry? You are the purpose for which I exist."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "– Tell me then: why is the process of image-formation, the sadhana (sādhana) of attaining the divine countenance, the sequential attainment of the relationship between image - murti (mūrti), image-bearer - pratima (pratimā) and the conditions of image creation - vigraha (vigraha). Why is such clear understanding hidden from souls in all manifested worlds? Why do they cling to the formlessness of their contrived-abstract Ego, like to a life raft in a stormy ocean, unaware that this raft is made of rotten reeds?"
Maha Vishnu: "– O my Source, Bhagavan, – He spoke, and his voice was like a quiet string stretched between worlds. – They have forgotten the rhythm of pratimāṃ dadhatī. The foundation of all existence is the imagery of higher cyclicity (para-cakra), that point where all religions converge."
Maha Vishnu: "– The Universe breathes. And this breathing is not a metaphor, but the essence of Bhagavan Kriya (Bhagavān Kriyā) practice. When inhalation and exhalation, prana (prāṇa) and apana (apāna), meet in conscious effort, directed against each other in sacred fusion, generating a state of neutralization and silence. In this silence, the first impulse of spiritual image is born."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "– Tell me about the source. About the nature of Anandamayi Ma (Ānandamayī Mā)."
Maha Vishnu: "– Anandamayi Ma is the very essence of bliss (ananda (ānanda)), – replied Maha Vishnu. – She is the luminous body whose nature is pure, causeless joy. From the light of her bliss, as from the reflection of the sun in a dewdrop, Ma generates your image, O Vishnu. You are the first template, the primordial sublimation, the archetype that will be manifested outwardly. Your birth is not an act of creation from nothing, but an act of reflection, image-formation from within outward. You are Name and Form, Nama and Murti, born from the Imaginal Bliss of Anandamayi Ma."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "– And this image becomes a matrix?"
Maha Vishnu: "– Yes. Just as a tree grows from a seed, and branches from a root, your image, O Vishnu, sublimates into my form – Maha Vishnu, the preserver of universes. But this sublimation occurs precisely as a consequence of your image immersing into the manifested world, thereby transforming the images emerging from Sushupti Shunyata - from the lunar well of Amara Kupa. You give meaning to all actions in the manifested world, guiding all beings toward their own image."
Maha Vishnu: "– The ultimate and most merciful of all images is the image of Sri Hari (Śrī Hari), which is revealed only to Anandamayi Ma herself, for Ma keeps this image within herself, and you are born precisely from it... but neither I nor you can even glimpse this image of Sri Hari (Śrī Hari)."
Maha Vishnu: "– You are the one who, in turn, expends your image into countless images of avatars (avatāra), descending into worlds to approach those born from Shunyata. And thus, the principle of higher and lower cyclicity comes into force. All inhabitants of manifested worlds, every soul, are called to form themselves into images of your avatars. This is not imitation, oh no. This is assimilation, sublimation of one's essence into higher essence, casting one's molten consciousness into a ready, divine form - Murti."

The wind of Vaikuntha brought the fragrance of night flowers, whose petals were woven from frozen prayers.

Bhagavan Vishnu: "– What about lineage, family? – asked Bhagavan. – Is it not an obstacle to such elevation?"
Maha Vishnu: "– On the contrary, lineage is the choice of one's future image, – explained Maha Vishnu. – The mechanism of marital sublimation templates (vivaha-sublimation) is divine alchemy. Husband and wife, through their love and mutual service, sublimate their images into each other, creating a unified energy field. Their children are born already within this field, inheriting not karmic burden, but accumulated potential of spiritually image-anchored sublimation. Thus, a clear ancestral scheme is built, where love is the energy, and family is the vessel in which this energy accumulates for the great purpose."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "– The purpose of final transfer?"
Maha Vishnu: "– Precisely so. The sublimation accumulated in the lineage is used for subsequent, final sublimation with higher images of the unmanifested, or, more accurately, the Beyond-manifested (Para-vyakta) being. What people call the 'afterlife' is merely a transition into this Beyond-manifested state. The soul's self-identification, its 'I-am-ness', is transferred from the perishable body and limited mind into the eternal body of light and cosmic consciousness. Lineage provides the necessary support for this leap."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "– But what prevents them from seeing this simplicity? Why is their breathing intermittent and unconscious?"
Maha Vishnu: "– Because their perception is clouded, O Preserver, – Maha Vishnu's voice became firmer. – Transparency of perception (vaisharada-pratibhasa (vaiśārada-pratibhāsa)) is the key. When perception is crystal clear, it resolves all contradictions of Ego (Ahaṃkāra) by itself. Modern humanity, especially since the beginning of Dvapara Yuga (Dvāpara Yuga) in 1700 according to Sri Yukteshwar's (Sri Yukteshwar) calculation, received a chance. The general cycle of all four Yugas, inscribed by Me into the great year of Earth's axis precession lasting 25,680 years, was given so that people, observing the slow dance of heavens, would realize the general structure of the manifested sphere. They were supposed to gradually sum up the acceleration of planetary and galactic bodies and understand that everything is a unified respiratory organism."
Bhagavan Vishnu: "– But they went a different way."
Maha Vishnu: "– They created harmful technologies and ideas that eclipsed the most important practice – the practice of breathing, Bhagavan Kriya. They seek salvation in the external, in the fast, in the loud. They replaced the inner sound 'SVA' – the sound of self-recognized 'I' (Sva – Self-recognized Atman) – with the deafening noise of the external world. And until they resolve the root causes of their internal contradictions, until each country establishes order in its own house, in the heart of its people, peace between countries will remain an unattainable illusion. Peace begins with the silence between inhalation and exhalation. With the realization that your true 'I' is not your thought, not your body, but the one who observes the breath. The one who is 'SVA'."
Revelation of the Divine Image

Silence fell, more eloquent than any words. And in this silence, the nature of Vaikuntha began to change. The first rays of the still invisible sun touched the edge of the black Moon, and for a moment it turned into a fiery ring – a reminder of cyclicity, that even great absorption is part of great birth. Stars and galaxies began to fade, not because the sky was lightening, but because space itself began to emit light from within. This was the light of awareness.

And then Bhagavan and Maha Vishnu saw the first mirrors of divine images. They appeared everywhere: in dewdrops on lotuses, in the smooth surface of waters, in the facets of crystals scattered along the shore. These mirrors reflected not only their divine countenances, but all contradictions, all fears, all delusions of all souls in all worlds. But now these contradictions were not chaotic. They were arranged in clear geometric patterns, they were presented, recognized, and, through this recognition, dissolved. This was the beginning of the great return – return to the source, to breath, to image.
